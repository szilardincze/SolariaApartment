Town and day trips

Sárvár is a compact, authentic Hungarian town that feels far from the crowds of Budapest or Lake Balaton. Reach everything on foot or by bike:

  • Nádasdy Castle — Renaissance castle and Nádasdy Ferenc Museum
  • Sárvár Arboretum (Várpark) — historic park beside the castle
  • Boating Lake (Csónakázó-tó) — islands, bridges, and walking paths
  • Day trips to Szombathely Cathedral and Sümeg Castle
